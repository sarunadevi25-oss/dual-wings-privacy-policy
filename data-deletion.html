Data Deletion Request

If you would like to request deletion of analytics data associated with your use of Dual Wings Uncaged, you can contact us using the email address below.

How to Request Data Deletion

Send an email to: dualwingsuncaged@gmail.com

Please mention "Data Deletion Request - Dual Wings Uncaged" in the subject of your email.

Request Data Deletion

What Data May Be Deleted?

Dual Wings Uncaged uses analytics to understand gameplay and improve the game. Analytics data may include anonymous or pseudonymous identifiers, gameplay and usage information, game version, and basic technical information.

We do not intentionally collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, videos, personal files, or other directly identifying personal information through the game.

Processing Your Request

After receiving your request, we will review it and take appropriate steps to process the request in accordance with applicable requirements and the capabilities of our analytics provider.

Because the game does not require an account, we may need additional information from you to help identify the relevant analytics data, where technically possible and appropriate.

Questions

If you have questions about data collected by Dual Wings Uncaged or about a deletion request, please contact us at dualwingsuncaged@gmail.com .
