Privacy Policy for Dual Wings Uncaged

Last Updated: October 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Dual Wings Uncaged ("the Game") handles information when you use the Game.

Dual Wings Uncaged is a mobile game developed and published independently.

By using the Game, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Dual Wings Uncaged does not require you to create an account and does not directly ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, password, payment information, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, or other personally identifying information.

The Game does not request access to your device's precise location.

However, third-party services used by the Game may automatically process certain technical, advertising, analytics, and usage information as described below.

1.1 Advertising — Google AdMob

The Game displays banner advertisements using Google AdMob, a service provided by Google.

To provide, measure, and improve advertising, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect or process information such as:

  • IP address
  • Android Advertising ID and other device or app identifiers, where available
  • App-related and device-related information
  • Information about interactions with advertisements and the Game
  • Diagnostic and performance information
  • Information used for advertising measurement and fraud prevention
  • Information used to provide and measure advertisements
  • General location information that may be inferred from an IP address

The Game does not request or collect your precise device location for advertising.

Google may use this information to provide, measure, personalize, and protect advertising services in accordance with Google's applicable policies and your available device or account privacy settings.

Google Mobile Ads SDK data is transmitted using encryption in transit.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, please visit: Google Privacy & Terms

1.2 Analytics — PostHog

The Game uses PostHog for product analytics and game-performance analysis.

PostHog may process information such as:

  • Game events and interactions
  • App usage information
  • Game session information
  • Device and application information
  • Diagnostic or performance information
  • An anonymous or pseudonymous identifier used to distinguish analytics events

Analytics data is used to understand how players interact with the Game, identify technical issues, improve gameplay, and improve the Game's performance and user experience.

The Game does not require you to provide an account or directly identify yourself to use the Game.

For more information about PostHog's privacy practices, please visit: PostHog Privacy Policy

2. Information We Do Not Collect Directly

Dual Wings Uncaged does not directly collect or request:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Passwords or account credentials
  • Contacts or address book information
  • Photos or videos
  • Microphone recordings
  • Precise device location
  • Payment card information
  • Bank information
  • Health information

The Game does not provide user accounts and does not currently offer in-app purchases.

3. Advertising

Dual Wings Uncaged currently displays banner advertisements using Google AdMob.

Advertising may involve the processing of device, advertising, interaction, diagnostic, and network information by Google and its advertising partners as necessary to provide and measure advertisements.

Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect information such as IP address, user interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers for advertising, analytics, and fraud-prevention purposes.

Advertising preferences and available controls may depend on your Android device settings, Google account settings, applicable regional requirements, and Google's advertising systems.

How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with the Game.

6. Data Retention

Analytics and advertising-related information may be retained by the relevant third-party service providers according to their respective retention policies and applicable legal requirements.

The Game developer does not maintain a separate user account database containing personal information.

7. Children's Privacy

Dual Wings Uncaged is a general-audience mobile game and is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

The Game does not knowingly request or collect personal information directly from children.

Parents or guardians who believe that a child has provided personal information through the Game should contact the developer so that the matter can be reviewed.

8. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your device, region, and applicable law, you may have choices regarding advertising identifiers, personalized advertising, and analytics.

You may be able to reset or delete your Android Advertising ID through your Android device's settings.

You may also uninstall the Game at any time to stop further collection of information through the Game.

9. Data Deletion Requests

Because Dual Wings Uncaged does not provide user accounts and does not maintain a personal user account database, there is no Game account to delete.

If you have a privacy-related request concerning information processed by a third-party service used by the Game, you may contact the developer using the contact information provided on the Game's Google Play listing.

Where appropriate, we will assist with directing the request to the relevant service provider.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the Game, its features, third-party services, or applicable legal requirements.

When changes are made, the Last Updated date at the top of this Privacy Policy will be updated.

You are encouraged to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

11. Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of Dual Wings Uncaged, please contact the developer using the contact information provided on the Game's Google Play listing.
