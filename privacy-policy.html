Privacy Policy for Dual Wings Uncaged

Effective Date: October 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Dual Wings Uncaged ("the Game", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the mobile game.

Dual Wings Uncaged is an Android mobile game. We are committed to collecting only the information necessary to operate, understand, and improve the Game.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Gameplay and Usage Information

The Game uses analytics to understand how players interact with the Game and to improve gameplay, performance, and user experience.

The analytics system may collect events and information such as:

  • When the Game is opened
  • When a game starts or restarts
  • Gameplay actions and whether actions are correct, incorrect, or missed
  • Completion of gameplay actions
  • Difficulty progression
  • Game-over events
  • Score and gameplay-related information associated with these events
  • Game version
  • Basic technical information associated with analytics events

The Game uses randomly generated identifiers for analytics purposes. These identifiers are not intended to directly identify you by name.

1.2 Device and Technical Information

Our analytics provider may automatically receive certain technical information associated with an analytics event, such as:

  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Screen dimensions
  • Browser or webview-related information used by the Game
  • Application and library information
  • Anonymous device and session identifiers
  • Basic information about the Game's runtime environment

The Game does not intentionally collect your device's IMEI, MAC address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, or GPS location.

The Game does not request permission to access your precise location.

2. Analytics Service

Dual Wings Uncaged uses PostHog for product and gameplay analytics.

PostHog helps us understand how players use the Game so that we can identify problems, understand gameplay behavior, and improve the Game.

The Game is configured to limit the information collected through analytics. In particular:

  • Person profiles are disabled.
  • Automatic event capture is disabled.
  • Session recording is disabled.
  • Heatmaps are disabled.
  • Surveys are disabled.
  • GeoIP enrichment is disabled.
  • Performance monitoring through PostHog is disabled.
  • Dead-click tracking is disabled.
  • Pageview and page-leave capture are disabled.

PostHog may process analytics data on our behalf in accordance with its applicable terms and privacy practices.

3. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

The Game does not intentionally collect or request:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Telephone number
  • Postal address
  • Contacts
  • Photos or videos
  • Personal files
  • GPS or precise location
  • IMEI
  • MAC address
  • Advertising ID
  • Payment card information
  • Health information
  • Biometric information
  • Government identification information

The Game also does not require you to create an account to play.

4. How We Use Information

Analytics information may be used to:

  • Understand how players interact with the Game
  • Monitor gameplay events
  • Identify gameplay problems and errors
  • Improve game mechanics and user experience
  • Understand general usage patterns
  • Evaluate game performance
  • Improve future versions of the Game

We do not use analytics information to sell personal information about players.

5. Advertising

The current version of Dual Wings Uncaged does not display advertisements.

If advertising is introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated as appropriate before or when that functionality is introduced.

6. In-App Purchases

The current version of Dual Wings Uncaged does not offer in-app purchases.

If in-app purchases are introduced in a future version, transactions will be processed through the applicable platform's payment system, such as Google Play Billing. We will update this Privacy Policy as appropriate.

7. Data Sharing

We use PostHog as an analytics service provider to process analytics information on our behalf.

We do not sell players' personal information.

We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide or maintain the Game
  • Use service providers that help operate the Game
  • Comply with applicable laws, regulations, legal processes, or valid governmental requests
  • Protect the security, rights, or property of the Game, its users, or others

8. Data Retention

Analytics information may be retained by our analytics service provider for the period necessary to provide analytics services and for legitimate business purposes, subject to the provider's applicable policies and retention practices.

We do not intentionally maintain a database containing players' names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, photos, or other directly identifying information through the Game.

9. Children's Privacy

Dual Wings Uncaged is a general-audience mobile game.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for the purpose of identifying them.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us using the contact information below so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed in connection with the Game. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Your Choices

You may stop using the Game at any time.

Depending on your device and Android version, uninstalling the Game may remove locally stored Game data and locally stored analytics identifiers. Information that has already been transmitted to our analytics provider may continue to be retained in accordance with applicable retention policies.

If you have questions about information associated with your use of the Game, you may contact us using the contact information below.
